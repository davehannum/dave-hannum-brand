MindDocket Privacy Policy

Draft for review. The release data inventory and retention details are still being verified.

Hannum Strategy LLC operates MindDocket. This policy describes how MindDocket handles information when you use the app and its share extension.

Account and library data

When you create or use an account, we process account information such as your email address and account identifier to sign you in, manage your session, and help you recover access. Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage for MindDocket.

Your library can include source links, captured text and media, notes, and the collections you use to organize them. If you use account backup, MindDocket sends library data and supported attachments to cloud storage so you can restore them.

Capturing and sharing content

When you capture a web or social post, MindDocket may send the source link to its servers to retrieve the available content and prepare a preview. If you choose to share a saved item with a connected AI service, the information you choose to send can include the source link, saved text, notes, and your instructions. The receiving service handles information it receives under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

You can remove saved items from your library. If you have a MindDocket account, you can initiate account deletion in the app. Contact us for help accessing or deleting your personal information.

Contact
