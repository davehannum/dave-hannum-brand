Enjoy my mind.Dave Hannum · Founder, MindDocket

My world has always been fitness, wellness, longevity, and the possibilities of connected technology.

For years, turning those possibilities into products meant finding capital, assembling teams, and having the staying power to keep going. Good ideas often waited.

AI has given me a creative rebirth. Experience and a curious mind can now become a working product in days or weeks. Building something that earns people’s trust still takes care—but the distance between imagining and making has changed.

MindDocket grew out of that feeling. I wanted a place for the things that sparked something: a workout, a recipe, a new approach to wellness, an idea for a business. Somewhere to keep the context and come back ready to use it.

This is one small piece of how my mind works. I hope it helps you do more with yours.

A few things to know

Curious?

What can I save?

MindDocket is built around ideas, workouts, recipes, and shopping finds, using links, images, and your own notes. What can be extracted depends on the source and the content available.

What happens when a post can’t be read?

Some sources restrict access. A link alone may not provide enough information. Adding screenshots or pasted text can provide the missing context; review extracted details before using them.

Does this replace ChatGPT or Claude?

No. MindDocket keeps your discoveries together and helps you take that context into the AI tools you already use. Their accounts and subscriptions are separate.

What will MindDocket cost?

MindDocket will be free at launch. There is no paid plan to choose here. Any future pricing will be communicated separately.

Can I download MindDocket now?

MindDocket is preparing for its iPhone launch. A verified App Store link will be added here when available.
