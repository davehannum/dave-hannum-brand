Before you start

A few things to know.

Does it capture every post perfectly?

No. Private posts, source restrictions and missing media can limit a capture. Review what was captured and add screenshots or pasted text when needed.

Can I change the section or add my own buckets?

Yes. Choose Ideas, Workouts, Exercises, Recipes or Shopping, then organize your saves into buckets within that section.

What is the difference between workouts and exercises?

A workout is a session to perform, with available exercises, repetitions and timing. An exercise is an individual movement to understand, keep for reference or try later.

Will it find a purchase link?

It keeps a captured purchase link when one is available. Some social posts only offer a creator profile, comment request or original post. Product identity, price and availability may need checking.

What does it cost?

MindDocket is free at launch. External AI services have their own accounts and terms. We are not offering a paid subscription on this page.

Is it available on the App Store?

MindDocket is preparing for its iPhone launch. A verified App Store link will appear here when available. The Ocean + Sky styling shown above is planned for the following update.
