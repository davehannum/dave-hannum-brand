The person behind the product

More life.
More possibility.

Being fit for life means being able to keep showing up—for a challenge, for the people I love, and for whatever comes next. My roots are in exercise physiology. My career has taken me through fitness equipment, connected experiences, product development, and building a company of my own.

I’ve seen what it takes to turn an idea into a business: capital, teams, patience, and staying power. That experience still matters. But AI has changed how quickly I can explore an idea and put something real in people’s hands.

This is where those worlds meet. A lifetime in fitness and wellness. A personal interest in longevity. And the energy of getting to build again.
