04 — The Story
Dave Hannum davehannum.com

I'm sixty-something, and I'm stronger, faster and more awake than I've ever been. That isn't a brag — it's the point.

I've spent thirty-plus years in product and sales across the fitness and wellness industry. I've seen the gear cycle through every fad, watched brands rise and fold, and helped a lot of good ones find the right buyer at the right time. I know what's built to last, and I know what's built for the catalog page.

A few years back, something shifted. Hyrox. Then Deka. Cold plunges in the morning, sauna at night, training with intent again. Not to look a certain way — to be a certain way. To stay in the arena for another twenty, thirty years.

That's what this site is about. The work. The protocols. The brands I help build. And the unglamorous truth that the best longevity strategy is showing up, hard, every day — in the heat and in the cold.

06 — The Moment

Built for this
moment.

For thirty years, bringing a serious wellness product to market meant teams, capital, gatekeepers and twenty-four-month cycles. That world is over.

What it took

The old way.

  • —A team of fifteen and a category buyer relationship.
  • —Eighteen months from concept to shelf.
  • —Capital to absorb three failed SKUs.
  • —Permission from people who didn't use the product.
What it takes now

The new way.

  • +Deep domain knowledge that can't be faked.
  • +Modern tools — AI, no-code, direct-to-audience.
  • +A body that actually runs the product.
  • +Speed: weeks, not quarters.
I.

The domain.

Thirty years of product, sales and category knowledge across fitness and wellness. The patterns that decide what sells — and what dies in a warehouse — are second nature.

30+ yrs hard-earned
II.

The practice.

I don't theorize about what works. I run it on a sixty-plus body, on race floors, in the sauna, in the plunge. The feedback loop is me.

60+ & competing lived, daily
III.

The toolkit.

AI, no-code, lean manufacturing, direct audience. I know the new landscape well enough to skip the middlemen and ship product myself — fast.

Weeks, not quarters new leverage

I'm not the founder who'll learn the industry on your dime. I'm not the technologist who'll guess at what matters in wellness. I've built a fitness-tech company and sold it. I'm the rare one in the middle. That's the leverage.
