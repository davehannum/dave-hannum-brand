— 01 · The Feature

An operator's view of the longevity space.

Most longevity advice is written by people who haven't lived it. I've spent thirty years inside the fitness and wellness industry — building the products, selling them, watching what works and what's marketing. Then, at sixty, I put it all on myself.

The training. The protocols. The races. The recovery. Hyrox. Deka. Cold plunge in the morning, sauna at night, training with intent again — not to look a certain way, but to be a certain way. To stay in the arena for another twenty, thirty years.

What you read here is what I've actually lived. No theory. No supplement stack I'm selling. Just three decades of pattern recognition and a body in its sixties that's stronger than it was at forty.

And the unglamorous truth that the best longevity strategy is showing up, hard, every day.
